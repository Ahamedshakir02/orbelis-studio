## 2. The problem

### Ideas that never get built. Problems that never get solved.

Every business has ideas it wants to bring to life and problems it needs to fix. What most lack is the technical team to deliver either.

**1. The idea that stays an idea**  
A new app, a booking system, a stronger website. The business can see the opportunity, but without the right team it remains on paper.

Enquiries answered too late, customers lost to faster competitors, processes that break as the business grows. Everyone knows the problem exists, but no one has the time or skills to solve it.

**3. The work no one should be doing**  
Re-entering data between forms, spreadsheets and WhatsApp. Answering the same questions every day. Hours lost every week to tasks that could run on their own.

## 10. FAQ

### Straight answers.

**What is Orbelis Studio?**  
Orbelis Studio is your own tech team, without the hiring. We are based in Edappal, Kerala, India, and work with clients in India and the UAE. We learn how your business runs, then build what it needs: SaaS products and web apps, websites and landing pages, brand identities, AI assistants and business automation, and the ads, SEO and social media that bring people in. Every project is scoped and quoted before work begins, and we can stay on each month after launch.

**What do you build?**  
Whatever the problem needs: a SaaS product or web app, a website or landing page, a brand, an AI assistant, automation between your tools, or the marketing that brings people in. You describe the problem, we suggest the smallest thing that solves it, and we say so when the answer is none of these.

**What does "your own tech team" mean?**  
It means we work the way an in-house team would. We learn how your business runs before suggesting anything, build in small steps you can see, and stay after launch to keep it working and build what comes next. You get the attention of an in-house team without having to hire one.

**How is a project priced?**  
To your requirements. There is no fixed price list, because a five-page site and a booking system with an assistant are not the same job. Send a short brief and you get a quote before any work starts, based on the number of pages, whether the site takes bookings, and whether it needs an assistant or automation. Applications are quoted stage by stage. Marketing work is quoted by the month, to the channels you want run.

**How long does it take?**  
About two weeks for a landing page or a small site, from agreed scope to launch. Larger sites take three to four weeks. Apps are built in stages, with a date agreed for each, and SEO builds over months. The schedule holds because scope is locked before work starts, not renegotiated halfway.

**Do you build SaaS products and web applications?**  
Yes. SaaS products, customer portals, internal tools, dashboards and mobile apps. A build starts with a first version real users can try, and grows in agreed stages from there.

**What can you automate?**  
The repetitive steps between your tools: following up an enquiry on WhatsApp or email, logging leads into a CRM or spreadsheet, sending reminders, moving invoices and approvals along, and compiling a weekly report. It starts with a short audit, and you are told plainly when something is not worth automating.

**Do I have to change the tools I already use?**  
No. What we build works with the tools your team already uses: your forms, spreadsheets, CRM, WhatsApp and email. Nobody has to move to something new.

**What is the AI assistant, in plain terms?**  
A chat window on your site that has read your own documents: prices, timings, policies, procedures. It answers from those rather than making things up, replies at any hour, and hands over to a person when a question is beyond it.

**Do you do marketing as well?**  
Yes. Meta and Google ads, SEO, social media management, and the creative design for all of them. They work best as one lead system: ads and search bring people in, the landing page turns them into enquiries, and automation follows each one up. You can also take any one of them on its own.

**Who pays for the ads?**  
You do, straight to Meta or Google from your own ad account, so you can see everything that is spent. Our fee is for running the campaigns and is quoted separately. We do not promise a number of leads; we report what was spent and what came in.

**Can I edit the site myself afterwards?**  
Yes. Sites whose content changes often come with an editor your team can use without touching code. For simpler sites, content changes are part of the care plan.

**Do I have to take the care plan?**  
No. The site is yours either way, and you can host it wherever you like. The care plan exists because sites age: content goes stale, software needs updating, the assistant needs new material. It is optional.

**Can you stay on as our tech team every month?**  
Yes. The tech team plan is a monthly plan with a set amount of building time each month, a monthly check-in on what to build next, and the care plan included. It is quoted to your business, like every project, and you can stop it at any time.

**What happens on the free consultation?**  
A 20-minute call. You tell us what you want to build or what is holding the business back, and you do not need to know which service you want. We come back with a clear recommendation: what we would build, what it would cost, and what is not worth doing.

**Where are you based? Do you work outside Kerala?**  
We are based in Edappal, Kerala, and work with clients across India and in the UAE. In Kerala we can meet in person; everywhere else, calls and reviews happen online, so you do not need to be near Edappal to work with us.

**Do you set up Google Business Profile and local SEO?**  
Yes. Local SEO is part of our SEO service: we set up or complete your Google Business Profile, keep your name, address and phone number the same everywhere they appear, and build the pages nearby customers search for. Results build over months, not days.

**Who does the work? Do you outsource it?**  
Everything is done in-house at Orbelis: design, build and marketing. Nothing is handed to an outside vendor you have never met, and one studio answers for the result.
